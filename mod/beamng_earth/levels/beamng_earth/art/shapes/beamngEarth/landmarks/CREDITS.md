Changes: converted to compiled static shapes, centered, scaled and oriented for geographic placement;
geometry decimated where needed, reduced distance LODs added, and materials/textures converted.
The Washington Monument retains its source plinth/materials with a new closed, tapered shaft based on NPS dimensions.
Each model's `landmark.json` records the precise transform, source checksum, dimension reference,
conversion warnings and geometry counts. Placement against the live terrain still requires in-game review.

The Pentagon remains procedural geometry from Overture/OpenStreetMap map data, not a downloaded model.
Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright),
with Overture's additional building-theme attribution listed in the repository's `CREDITS.md`.
